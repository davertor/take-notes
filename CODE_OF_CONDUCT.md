# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is at that link; the short version is what it always is:

- Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith.
- Critique the code, the notes, or the idea — never the person.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not welcome here.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing this project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **davertor@gmail.com**.
Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit, delete, or block, and
will explain the decision when asked.
